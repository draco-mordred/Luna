Luna Music v1.0.1

Highlights
- Added a custom Luna app icon across the app and Windows installer.
- Smoothed PlayBar transitions and improved lyric layout to keep lyrics clear of track information and controls.
- Added synchronized lyrics visibility controls for the PlayBar and Now Playing views.
- Added Windows system-accent color support, with an option to keep a custom accent instead.
- Centered PlayBar lyrics responsively and added a first-launch release notes dialog for each app version.
- Updated the Windows installer with Luna branding, a language selector, install-folder selection, and replacement confirmation.
- Improved responsive library browsing and the customizable player visualizer.

Notes
- Local music folder scanning is available in the Electron desktop app.
- Audiomack streaming requires authorized API credentials and is available in the desktop app.
- Jamendo access requires a Client ID. Online catalogs and streams remain subject to their providers' terms and licensing.
